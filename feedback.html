What parents say about Makkala Mane.

We are grateful for the trust parents place in us and for the kind words they share about their children's experiences.

Makkala Mane is a wonderful and safe place for parents looking for a trustworthy daycare while they are at work. The caring and dedicated staff give individual attention to every child, helping them feel comfortable, happy, and well cared for. The centre maintains excellent cleanliness and hygiene, with regular sanitization of toys and surroundings, along with a good variety of educational and engaging toys that support learning and development. The teachers are approachable, caring, and maintain good communication with parents, keeping them well informed about their child. Overall, I am very happy with Makkala Mane and truly appreciate the safe, clean, caring, and child-friendly environment. I would definitely recommend Makkala Mane to parents looking for a reliable daycare for their children.

Madhu KMParent Review

Starting school is a big step—for both kids and parents. We feel incredibly lucky to have chosen "Makkala Mane", Bolwar, Puttur as our son’s first playschool. From day one, the teachers and care takers have been incredibly kind, supportive, and attentive—helping our child feel safe, confident, and happy.

Here’s what makes Makkala Mane truly special:

✅ Warm, nurturing environment

✅ Dedicated teachers and caretakers who support both academic and emotional growth of child

✅ A healthy balance of physical activities, creative play, festive celebrations, and cultural values that enrich a child's early years

✅ Nutritious, child-friendly meals

✅ Great focus on hygiene and health, especially important for young toddler

✅ Meaningful birthday celebrations for every child—marked by a traditional Aarati, heartfelt Sanskrit birthday chants, heartfelt moments that make every child feel truly special (beyond just cake-cutting!)

✅ Enriching field visits to places like the railway station, police station, fire safety office, parks, and more—helping children explore the real world and learn through experience.

✅ Convenient timings from 8:45 AM to 6:00 PM—a blessing for working parents

▪️ If you're searching for the right place for your toddler’s first school experience, Makkala Mane is truly the best choice you can make.

Thank you Poornima Valthaje and the entire team for making this beginning so beautiful!
